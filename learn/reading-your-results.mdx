In this module we show you what the dashboard is telling you, from one person through to the shape of your whole user base.

## Understanding your data

* **Activity** is everything that happened in VerifyYou, newest first. **Sources** lists the files you imported and the HumanChecks people arrive through, and **People** lists everyone, sorted with the most abnormal first. See [Reading Activity](/docs/activity/reading-activity)
* Filter **People** by source, by a column from your own file, or by **Signals** set to **Anything abnormal** for the ones already worth a look
* **Insights** rolls the same data up across whichever period you pick: **settled passes**, every run that reached a decision; **liveness pass rate**, who proved a live face on capture; and **elevated risk**, how many people scored into the elevated or high bands. See [Reading Insights](/docs/insights/reading-insights)
* **Threat levels over time** stacks your scored sessions by day, and **top threat families** shows what led the score, which tells you whether one person is behind it or many
* Your own systems can ask the same question without anybody opening the dashboard. Send an email or phone to [/v3/confirmations](/v3/dev/spec/server-api/lookup) and you get back where that person stands with you

## One person in full

* Click any row and you get that person: an estimated age range, the HumanChecks they took and how each ended, the batches they arrived in, the columns from your file, and the email, phone or face we hold for them. See [the person page](/docs/activity/guest-profiles)
* **Your signals** are the seven you picked, shown on every person and in every batch. We read 31 for everyone, grouped as Identity, Connection, Device, Email, Behavior and Passive, and you choose and order your picks in **Settings**, under **Signals**
* Each signal reads as pending, no data, normal, or abnormal, and an abnormal one tells you whether we calculated it or observed it directly. See [Threat levels and signals](/docs/insights/threat-levels-and-signals)

## Who is arriving, and how

* **Session attributes** shows where sessions come from, with the top five countries, platforms, device types and browsers
* **People** covers who verifies here: liveness passed against failed, estimated age, and gender
* **Risk flags** gives the share of people arriving with each network or automation flag raised, across VPN, datacenter IP, Tor exit, abuse-listed IP, TLS automation and fingerprint mismatch
