In this module we show you how Passive Signals reads the visits to your site, before anyone takes a HumanCheck. It is in beta, and the findings come to you by email.

To get it running on your site, we recommend the following:

## Add your site

* Open **Passive Signals** in the left nav and click **Add a site**. There is nothing to configure beyond the site itself
* Paste the tag inside the `<head>` of your pages and deploy. **Show the install** brings the snippet back whenever you need it
* Until we see the tag on a deployed page, the site shows **Looking for the tag**. Once traffic arrives it changes to **Receiving**, with the date it started
* You can add more than one site, and each carries its own status

## What we read

* We read how each visit behaves: the mouse path, clicks, keystroke timing, how long keys are held, and how fast forms are filled in
* It runs silently. Your visitors see no prompt and no banner, and there is nothing for them to click
* A visit is read within its first few actions on the page

## What you get during the beta

* While Passive Signals is in beta, the dashboard page shows your sites and their status, and no charts yet
* The findings arrive as a **Traffic report** in your email: how many visits to each of your sites were humans and how many were agents, page by page
* Nothing on your site changes because of it. Passive Signals reads and reports, and every visitor carries on as normal

## Where HumanCheck fits

* Passive signals run on everyone, so the face check only needs to come in where the risk is. Put an anonymous HumanCheck in front of the pages your report shows agents reaching, or the actions that pay out. See [Create a HumanCheck](/learn/create-a-verification)
* People who take a HumanCheck also carry a **Passive** group among their signals, marked beta. You choose which signals show on every person in **Settings**, under **Signals**
