Create your own workspace in a few steps, or [book a demo](https://sales.verifyyou.com) and we set it up with you.

## Create your account

Go to [platform.verifyyou.com/sign-up](https://platform.verifyyou.com/sign-up). There are five steps, and the counter in the corner shows where you are.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your work email">
    Type the address you want to sign in with and click **Continue**. If that address already has an account, we tell you to sign in instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code we email you">
    We send a 6-digit code to that address. If it does not arrive, **Resend** unlocks after a short countdown, and **Use a different email** takes you back to fix a typo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a password">
    You sign in with this from now on. It needs at least 12 characters, and we turn down any password that has turned up in a known data breach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take a HumanCheck yourself">
    Click **Start verification** and look at your camera for a few seconds. It is the same check your people will take, so you see it from their side before anyone else does. If your camera is blocked, or you would rather do it later, click **Skip for now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name yourself and your workspace">
    Enter your full name and the name of the company the workspace is for, then click **Create workspace**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You land on **Activity**, signed in, with an empty workspace that is yours. If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from the account menu, which opens from your name.

## Create your first HumanCheck

Open **HumanChecks** and create your first one. The setup walks five steps:

1. **Get started** introduces what a HumanCheck does.
2. **Pick your check** offers presets for common jobs, then asks whether people reach it with **No-code** or **Code**.
3. **Branding** puts your logo and company name on the check.
4. **Tune it** sets how strict the check is, with a preview beside the settings.
5. **Integrate** finishes the setup and opens the new HumanCheck.

A preset is a starting point. Two companies running a signup gate often want different answers on how many times one person may pass and what a duplicate should cost, so go through [Configuration](/docs/verifications/configuration) afterwards and make each setting match what you are protecting. [Creating a verification](/docs/verifications/creating-a-verification) covers the wizard in more depth.

## Try it before anyone else does

Open the HumanCheck, go to **Integration**, and click **Run a test**. It runs the real check on test data with a test identity, so nothing touches your live records. Fail it on purpose too, so you have seen what your people see when it goes wrong.

## Put it in front of people

No code gets you started with zero developer effort: share the HumanCheck's link, email it to people you already have, or put one-time passwords in front of a survey. Code puts the check inside your own product with the SDK, and your server confirms the result. Your workspace has test and live keys from the start. See [Integration](/docs/verifications/integration) for every method, and [Send verifications by email](/docs/guides/existing-users) to reach an existing list.

## Bring your team in

From **Team**, invite colleagues by email and pick a role for each. See [Team and roles](/docs/workspace/team-and-roles).

## Understand your data

**Activity** lists everyone who took a check or came in on an import, and opening a row gives you everything we saw about that person. **Insights** rolls it up across a period, so you see the shape of all your traffic at once. See [Reading Activity](/docs/activity/reading-activity) and [Reading Insights](/docs/insights/reading-insights).
