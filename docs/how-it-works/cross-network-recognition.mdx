Every HumanCheck runs on one shared network. When somebody passes a check on any platform using HumanCheck, their face vector is on the network, so the next platform can recognise that person and see if they have been flagged. Every platform that joins makes the network of verified humans stronger, and everyone in it better protected.

## What it gives you

* A person verified elsewhere can be recognized on your check without scanning again
* You still get the same verdict, a real and unique human, without paying for their friction
* If we have seen that person flagged somewhere else in the network, we tell you

## What decides how much they are asked

Your own configuration decides. Whether a recognized person walks straight through, re-verifies on a cadence, or scans every time comes from your **Verification limit** and **Skip check on known devices**. See [Configuration](/docs/verifications/configuration).

A stricter setting on your platform changes nothing about anyone else's, and theirs changes nothing about yours.

<Note>
  Skipping the scan saves the person friction, not you a verification. A returning guest who passes without scanning still counts as one.
</Note>
