Verify the people you already have: set up the address your invites come from, import your list, and send each person a HumanCheck link that belongs to them alone. There is no mail system on your side to wire up, because we host the sending. If you do not have a workspace yet, [the Start guide](/docs/get-started/getting-access) walks you through creating one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have a HumanCheck that takes a link">
    Any HumanCheck set to **Sharing a link** or **Existing users** can go out by email. One that asks for a code, through **With code** or **One-time passwords**, cannot, because an emailed link has nothing to start it with.

    If you do not have one yet, open **HumanChecks** and create one. [Creating a verification](/docs/verifications/creating-a-verification) walks the setup, and [Branding your check](/docs/verifications/branding-your-check) puts your logo on it.

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> a HumanCheck your people can open from an email.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the address your invites come from">
    Go to **Connectors**, open **Email**, and stay on the **Senders** tab.
    1. Click **Add a sender** and fill in the **From address**, a **Display name**, and a **Reply-To** if you want replies to land somewhere else.
    2. We email that address a confirmation link. Nothing sends from it until the owner of the inbox clicks through.
    3. To send from your own domain, publish the records listed under **Your own domain** with your DNS provider. Each one flips from **Pending** to **Live** once we see it.

    <Warning>
    Until your domain's records are live, Microsoft inboxes refuse mail from it, so Outlook, Hotmail and Live addresses are not reached. Finish DNS before a large batch.
    </Warning>

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> a confirmed From address, with your own domain on the way.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    On the **Templates** tab. There are two kinds of message: **Initial invite** goes to people who have not verified with you yet, and **Re-verification** goes to people who have.
    1. Open a template, or create one, and write the subject and body in your own words.
    2. Keep the **HumanCheck link** chip in the body. Each send turns it into the personal link of whoever receives it, and a template without it will not save.
    3. Click **Save template**. One template can serve several HumanChecks.

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> the words your people will read.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair a sender and a template with your HumanCheck">
    On the **Assignments** tab. Each HumanCheck gets one **Sender**, one **Invite template** and one **Re-verify template**. If you leave the re-verify template alone, people who verified before get the invite too.

    The send step uses these pairings exactly as they stand, so this is where you change them.

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> everything a send needs, set once for every future send.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your people">
    Go to **Activity** and click **Import**.
    1. Drop a CSV, up to 2 MB, or paste rows straight in. Whole spreadsheet rows are fine.
    2. Match your columns to ours. Only the email column is required. **Amount**, **Date** and **IP address** land on each person's record where you can compare them later, and an IP address you hold sits beside the one we see when that person verifies. Every other column comes along as extra data.
    3. Name the import, or add these people to an earlier one. The name becomes a tag you can find them by later.
    4. Click **Import**, with the number of people you are adding on the button.

    People are matched by email, so somebody already in your workspace is updated and tagged, never duplicated. A **Tasks** card in the corner shows the import while we look each person up, and **Open** takes you to the import's page when it is done. Importing never sends anything, so you can add to the list over several sittings.

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> your people in, under one tag, and nothing sent.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    On the import's page, click **Send to all**, which carries the number of people. To send to one person instead, open their profile and click **Send HumanCheck**.

    The **Send HumanCheck** sheet shows who it reaches by email, the **From** address, the **Message** each group gets (first-timers get Initial invite and returning people get Re-verification), and a **Preview**, with **Edit template** if you want one more pass. It also tells you up front who will not be reached and why, for example addresses with no email or Microsoft addresses waiting on DNS. Click **Send to** and the number.

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> invites going out, each one a link locked to its recipient.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the results come in">
    The import's page sorts everyone into tabs as they come through: **Done**, **Opened, not completed**, **Never opened**, **Not reached** and **Not sent yet**. So you can see who started and stopped, and who never opened the link at all.

    Each person also carries the state of their email, from **Queued** and **Sent** to **Delivered**, or **Bounced** when it fails, and **Verified** once they finish the check. [Reading Activity](/docs/activity/reading-activity) covers what to do with what arrives.

    <p className="vy-have"><strong>You now have:</strong> results arriving as each person verifies. That's the whole setup.</p>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Sending limits.** Each sender has a daily send budget. Anything past it waits for the next day's budget, and the send summary tells you how many are waiting. If bounce or spam rates from a sender cross our safety line, sending from it pauses, and the sender shows why. Email is the only channel today: phone numbers in your file import as extra data, and SMS sending is not available yet.
</Note>
