Insights sorts your sessions into threat bands, from **Low** to **High**. A person's own signals read as pending, no data, normal, or abnormal rather than as a score. See [Comprehensive trust score](/docs/activity/guest-profiles) for how signals read on one person.

## The threat bands

Insights groups sessions for the population view into four bands: **Low**, **Moderate**, **Elevated**, **High**. Higher means more of concern. Each band is built from the same scoring that runs on every check, rolled up across whichever period you pick.

## The threat families

**Top threat families** on Insights groups what fired into five families: **Network**, **Velocity**, **Automation**, **Liveness** and **Identity**.

**Network** is where the connection came from: a VPN, a datacenter, a Tor exit, an IP already known for abuse.

**Velocity** is how fast or how often something is happening, such as a run of checks in quick succession.

**Automation** is whether the session behaves like a real browser or like a script, read from how the connection presents itself.

**Liveness** is the capture itself: failed attempts, low confidence, a screen held up to the camera.

**Identity** is about the credential behind the person.

Which family dominates tells you what you are dealing with, and it is a different question from how much of it there is.
