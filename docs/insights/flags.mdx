The **Flags** tab has been retired. Reviewing who your checks caught now happens in three places instead of one list.

## Where it moved

Filter **Activity** to **Signals: Anything abnormal** to start from the people worth a look, sorted most abnormal first. Open a person to see the signal behind the flag, every HumanCheck they took, and any other accounts their face or device is tied to. See [Reading Activity](/docs/activity/reading-activity).

**Add to audit** sets people aside from any list, so your team can review, export or send to them together from **Audits**.

An account collision (one face on more than one account) is still a configuration decision: [Configuration](/docs/verifications/configuration) sets what happens when one is found.
