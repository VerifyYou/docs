[**Team**](https://platform.verifyyou.com/dashboard/team) is where your colleagues get access to the workspace.

Invite by email. The member list shows everyone with access, and **Remove** takes somebody off.

Access is per workspace, so anybody you add can see every verification in it, along with the Activity and Insights behind them. There is no per-verification access, which matters if one of your checks gates something the whole team should not be reading.

Our team creates your workspace. You invite your own people once you are in. See the [start guide](/docs/get-started/getting-access).
